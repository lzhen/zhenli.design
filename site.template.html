DLS Magician explores a more connected way to build: describe what should happen, make the logic explicit, and bring an interface into the conversation.

01 — Our approach
Better tools don't just
generate. They help us understand.


01 ◌
Start with people.
A useful system begins with what someone needs to do, not what the technology can produce.


02 ⌘
Make complexity legible.
Connect intent, decisions, and behavior so product, design, and engineering can work from a shared understanding.


03 ↗
Keep people in control.
Make room to inspect, question, and refine. Clarity should support judgment, not replace it.




02 — Why Empathie
