ProblemDeck 06

How might AI simplify complex ad workflows to build user trust?

Manual campaign setup requires advertisers to navigate multiple tools and technical decisions.

  1. 01 / 2 stepsSet up campaign

    Begin the advertising workflow.

  2. 02 / 2+ stepsConfigure conversion & tracking

    Choose what to measure.

  3. 03 / 3+ stepsConnect a data source

    Complete the integration.

  4. 04 / 3+ stepsImprove data quality

    Resolve signal issues.

  5. 05 / 1 stepPublish campaign

    Return and launch.

10+ steps across the manual workflow shown in the deck.
1

Generic guidance

Without account and Signals context, the assistant risked giving advice that was technically validated but not relevant.

2

Fragmented setup

Recommendations often pointed to another product, adding more hops before an advertiser could complete the task.

3

Low confidence

Advertisers needed to understand why a method was recommended, what would change, and whether the action succeeded.

Advertiser journeyDeck 09

A reusable mental model made AI guidance actionable.

  1. 01 / DefineBusiness goals

    Identify what the advertiser wants to achieve and the actions that align with those goals.

  2. 02 / AlignBusiness partners

    Align the internal team on what to track and the constraints on data collection.

  3. 03 / DecideSetup strategy

    Review connection options and choose the best setup method.

  4. 04 / SetupData sharing

    Implement data sharing and verify that the setup works.

  5. 05 / TroubleshootPerformance issues

    Detect and resolve problems that hinder data sharing.

  6. 06 / MaximizeAd performance

    Improve campaign decisions and signal quality over time.

New advertiserDefine → Align → Decide → Setup → Troubleshoot

Existing advertiserMaximize → Align → Decide (optional) → Setup (optional) → Troubleshoot
