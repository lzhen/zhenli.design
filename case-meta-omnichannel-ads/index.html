Omnichannel Ads

Connect online and in-store outcomes — and make data readiness part of the campaign journey.

Ads Manager and Events Manager.

01 / The problem

A campaign option is only useful when the data is ready.

Retailers needed to optimize for purchases online and in store. But selecting an omnichannel goal was only one part of the experience: advertisers also needed to connect offline data, understand its quality, and return to campaign setup with confidence.

My work focused on the Signals foundation underneath that campaign experience. The design challenge was to make dependencies visible without asking advertisers to understand every backend detail.

02 / The journey

Follow the advertiser across product boundaries.

I mapped the relationship between campaign decisions, data connection, quality improvement, and optimization. This helped frame the work as one advertiser journey rather than separate features owned by separate surfaces.

  1. 01 / Set upBegin the campaign in Ads Manager.
  2. 02 / ConfigureChoose conversion and tracking settings.
  3. 03 / ConnectSet up the data source in Events Manager.
  4. 04 / ImproveResolve quality gaps and return to the campaign.

The principle was to build on familiar workflows, explain what was needed at the moment of need, and keep guidance consistent across Ads Manager and Events Manager.

03 / Key decisions

Make a complex system feel like one experience.

01 / Campaign setup

Extend the familiar campaign flow.

Place online-and-in-store options alongside existing conversion settings. Avoid asking advertisers to learn a disconnected setup model just to use a new campaign capability.

02 / Readiness guidance

Explain why an option is unavailable.

Make the missing requirement understandable and guide advertisers toward the next step, instead of leaving them with an unexplained disabled choice.

03 / Offline quality

Reuse a familiar diagnostic pattern.

Extend the score-and-recommendation experience in Events Manager to offline signals. Connect quality feedback with a useful next action.

Across the journey

Connect the fix back to the goal.

Keep the campaign objective visible while advertisers work on data readiness. Integration and quality are steps toward using the campaign, not isolated technical tasks.

04 / Program outcomes

A shipped foundation for omnichannel growth.

The broader Omnichannel Ads program connected online and offline signals within one campaign product. Signals design contributed to that launch alongside product, engineering, data, and other design teams.

Program-level figures reported in my portfolio presentation. They describe the broader launch, not revenue generated by my design alone or a causal estimate of the Signals UX contribution. Annual run rate is not a claim of recurring subscription revenue.

05 / Reflection

Design the path to readiness, not just the final screen.

The important interaction was the connection between campaign intent and usable signals. A clear setup screen could not compensate for confusing data requirements or a dead end in another tool.

This work strengthened how I approach platform design: make system dependencies legible, reuse trusted interaction patterns, and keep the user’s goal intact as they cross product boundaries.
