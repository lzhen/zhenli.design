Meta Omnichannel Ads / Offline Signals

Maximize sales online and in store using one campaign.

A 0–1 journey from offline data integration to quality measurement, campaign enablement, and value expression.

Role
Product Design DRI for the Signals team. Partnered with 6+ designers across 4+ organizations.
Scope
Led Omnichannel / Offline Signals strategy through execution: data integration, quality measurement, campaign enablement, and value expression.
Outcome context
15% lower median cost per omni purchase on average. 21% higher store incremental return on ad spend. Results reported in the deck.

PrinciplesDeck 26

Make a complex system feel like one experience.

Build on familiar workflows, provide guidance where needed, and keep patterns consistent.

1

Respect familiar workflows

Build on the conversion flows advertisers already understand.

2

Guide users at the moment of need

Make requirements and next steps clear without breaking the journey.

3

Minimize unnecessary differences

Reuse familiar patterns when the underlying concepts are the same.

Ads Manager → Events Manager → Ads ManagerOmnichannel campaign → Data quality score → Improvement instructions → Return to the campaign

SolutionsDeck 27–29

Carry the same principles across product surfaces.

Solution 1

Respect familiar workflows

Build Omnichannel into the existing campaign flow.

Place online-and-in-store options alongside familiar conversion settings.

Solution 2

Guide users at the moment of need

Explain what advertisers need at the moment they need it.

Show why an option is unavailable and guide advertisers to the next step.

Solution 3

Minimize unnecessary differences

Extend the familiar data quality experience to offline signals.

Use the existing score-and-recommendation pattern in Events Manager.
